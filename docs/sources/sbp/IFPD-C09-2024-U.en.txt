IFPD Circular No. 9 of 2024                                   26 November 2024

To: Presidents / Chief Executives, all Islamic banks and conventional banks with Islamic banking branches

Subject: Provision of profit on savings deposits of Islamic banking institutions

1. It has been decided that Islamic banking institutions shall pay profit on rupee savings deposits
(excluding the deposits of financial institutions, public sector enterprises and public limited companies)
at a rate of at least 75 percent of the weighted average gross yield of all pools of the Islamic banking
institution.

2. To determine the gross yield of each pool, the monthly gross income of the pool shall be divided by the
monthly average assets of the pool (excluding fixed assets). However, for the purpose of determining the
weighted average gross yield of pools, the pools created by Islamic banking institutions for the Shariah
compliant standing ceiling facility and for Shariah compliant open market operations (OMOs) shall not be
counted.

3. Accordingly, the following amendments are made to the instructions "Profit and Loss Distribution and Pool
Management for Islamic Banking Institutions" issued under IBD Circular No. 3 dated 19 November 2012 and
IBD Circular Letter No. 1 dated 1 January 2013:
(i) Clause 4.2.3 shall be deleted.
(ii) Clause 5.2.1 is amended to read: "If the pool's income is lower than market expectations, Islamic
banking institutions may forgo a part of their Mudarib share as Hiba so that market expectations can be
met. However, Islamic banking institutions that maintain a Profit Equalisation Reserve (PER) may reduce
their Mudarib share only when the PER is insufficient to improve the profit paid to depositors."
(iii) Clause 5.2.2 is amended to read: "Islamic banking institutions may, if needed, provide Hiba to savings
account depositors to meet the minimum profit rate."

4. These instructions shall come into force from 1 January 2025.
